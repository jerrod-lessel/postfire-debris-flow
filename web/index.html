How this was made

Sentinel-2 imagery gives burn severity, a 10 m USGS elevation model gives slope and drainage, and the USDA soil survey gives erodibility. Those three feed the USGS M1 model, which returns the rainfall each basin needs for a 50% chance of producing a debris flow.

The model implementation matches the official USGS package exactly, and reproduces the published USGS assessment for this fire to floating point precision when given their inputs. Compared against a field-validated soil burn severity map, this pipeline runs conservative: it over-warns rather than under-warns, on this fire.
