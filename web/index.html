Scar Threshold

How hard it has to rain before a burned canyon lets go

Point at a basin to see its numbers.

How this was made

Sentinel-2 imagery gives burn severity, a 10 m USGS elevation model gives slope and drainage, and the USDA soil survey gives erodibility. Those three feed the USGS M1 model, which returns the rainfall each basin needs for a 50% chance of producing a debris flow.

The model implementation matches the official USGS package exactly, and reproduces the published USGS assessment for the Bridge Fire to floating point precision when given their inputs. Compared against field-mapped soil burn severity, this pipeline runs conservative on both fires here: it over-warns rather than under-warns.

"High" means at least a 60% chance, which is where the top two of the five likelihood classes USGS publishes begin.
